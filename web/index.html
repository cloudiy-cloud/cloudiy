How it works

Install the Node

2
Contribute Resources
CPU, memory, GPU and storage. Share what you choose, keep the rest private.
3
Declare Workloads
Say what you need: resources and capabilities. Never Docker, drivers or servers.
4
Get Signed Results
Isolated execution, cryptographic proof of origin, settled in USDC on Solana.
